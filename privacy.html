Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This page explains how MuskySocials handles information when you connect a third-party social account or use a social-publishing feature. The operator must review and complete this policy before relying on it as the full privacy policy for the service.

Before publication: replace the support-contact placeholder below and review this policy against all data collected by the live MuskySocials service.

Information we handle

  • Account information: information associated with your MuskySocials account and the social account you choose to connect, such as a provider user ID, display name, and profile image.
  • Authorization data: access and refresh tokens and permission scopes needed to maintain the connection and perform actions you request. Social tokens are encrypted on the server and are not intended to be exposed to your browser.
  • Your content: drafts, captions, hashtags, media files you upload, publishing preferences, and publishing status identifiers needed to provide the requested tools.
  • Technical and security information: limited request and error information used to protect the service, troubleshoot failures, and prevent duplicate publishing actions.

How we use information

We use this information to operate MuskySocials, save your drafts, suggest content and hashtags, connect the social account you select, submit posts only after you request and confirm the action, check publishing status, maintain security, and provide support.

Third-party social platforms

When you connect an account, the relevant provider (for example, TikTok) receives authorization information and content necessary to perform the action you approved. Your use of that platform is also subject to its own terms and privacy policy. Connecting an account does not, by itself, publish content.

Sharing and selling

MuskySocials does not sell your social-account access tokens. Information is shared with the service providers needed to host MuskySocials, store data securely, authenticate your account, and perform a social-publishing action you request. The operator must ensure this description matches the service's actual providers and practices before publication.

Storage, security, and retention

Social authorization tokens are encrypted server-side. We use access controls intended to limit account data to the relevant user and authorized server functions. No internet service can promise absolute security. We retain account connections, drafts, media, and publishing records while needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational or legal needs, or resolve disputes. You may disconnect a social account in MuskySocials; disconnecting prevents future use of that connection but may not delete posts already published on the external platform.

Your choices

You can choose whether to connect a social account and must confirm before the publishing flow sends content to the provider. You can disconnect an account through MuskySocials. To request access to or deletion of information, contact the service operator using the support contact listed below, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

Children

MuskySocials is not intended for people who are below the minimum age required by the service or the connected social platform.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when a material update is made.
