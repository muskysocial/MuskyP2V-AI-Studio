STAY IN CONTROL

Privacy essentials

🔑
Keep sign-in details private

Use a unique password, never share verification codes, and avoid entering passwords into links sent by strangers.

📷
Check before sharing media

Only upload photos, videos, documents, or voice recordings you intend to share with the selected feature.

🎙️
Device permissions

Camera and microphone access should begin only when you use the relevant controls. You can review or revoke site permissions in your browser settings.

🌐
External AI tools

Some creator tools open third-party services. Those services may receive media you choose to submit and have their own privacy terms.

!
Know what is and isn't available

This screen provides guidance and shortcuts; it does not itself change account visibility, block/report users, export your data, or delete your account. Contact support for help with a privacy request. Do not share passwords or payment secrets in a support message.
